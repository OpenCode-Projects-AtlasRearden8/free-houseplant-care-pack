Watering Rotation Sheet

One steady rhythm, and every plant shows up fed.

Getting started

Watering dies not from neglect but from chaos — one plant gets double helpings, another waits three weeks. This sheet gives you a soft rotation: water a few plants a day, mark the box, and the whole collection stays even without one heroic weekend marathon.

Print it right

  • US Letter paper, portrait, 100 % scale (“Actual size”, never “Fit to page”).
  • Print one per month and clip it to a fridge, a corkboard, or the watering can.
  • Keep a pencil with it — a sheet you cannot reach while holding the can is a sheet that goes unfilled.

How the rotation works

  • Write each thirsty plant in a row, with its target day (Monday for the ferns, and so on).
  • On the way past, check the soil with a finger before pouring — damp soil means skip.
  • When you water, tick Done and pencil in the date. When a row is full, print a fresh sheet.
Tip: Under 10 plants? Give them one shared watering day and use the Done column as a roll call. Over 20? Split the herd — Mon/Wed group A, Tue/Fri group B — and this sheet carries it.

The three-finger rule

Push a finger two knuckles into the soil. Bone dry — water thoroughly until it drains. Evenly damp — skip, check again tomorrow. Soggy days after watering — the pot may be rootbound or the drainage is clogged; turn the pot and loosen the saucer.
