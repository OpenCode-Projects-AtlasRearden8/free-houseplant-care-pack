Monthly Plant-Sitter Card

Leave it on the counter. Help your sitter win the week.

Getting started

The kindest gift you can give a friend watering your plants is not gratitude — it is instructions. This single-page card tells them which plants need what, when the watering can lives, and the one sentence that saves entire collections: when in doubt, skip the watering. Tuck it under a cup on the kitchen counter and everyone relaxes.

Print it right

  • US Letter paper, portrait, 100 % scale (“Actual size”, never “Fit to page”).
  • Fill it in before you leave — not from the airport, from the hallway, where all the plants are.
  • Leave it where the plants are, not where the return key is.

What your sitter actually needs

  • A go/no-go test: check the soil two knuckles deep; damp means skip.
  • Where the watering can and anything fiddly live (misters, saucers, the trick door).
  • One contact number for the one plant that matters most.
Tip: Group the thirstiest plants in one warm, bright spot before you go. One corner of care replaces five trips down the hall — and the card then says “water the corner,” which any overbooked friend can manage.

Golden rule: stick a finger two knuckles into the soil — damp means skip, dry means a thorough drink until water leaves the drainage hole. When in doubt, skip the watering. Underwatered plants forgive; overwatered ones do not.
