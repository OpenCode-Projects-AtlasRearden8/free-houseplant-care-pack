Plant Profile Cards

Get to know each of your leafy roommates.

Getting started

Every plant is a little different — one drinks weekly, another sulks in dim corners and nurses a grudge. Fill in one of these cards for each plant and you will always know what it wants, even months from now when watering day and memory have drifted apart. This is exactly the information a plant-sitter needs, too.

Print it right

  • US Letter paper, portrait, 100 % scale (choose “Actual size” — never “Fit to page”).
  • Plain paper works fine. Card stock is nicer if the card will live next to the pot.
  • Cut the sheet into eight cards, or slip the whole page into a plastic sleeve.

Make it yours

  • One card per plant. Write the botanical name or a fond nickname — both work.
  • Circle the light level you actually measured, not the level you hope for.
  • Update “last watered” in pencil so the card stays honest without getting messy.
Tip: If you cannot decide on a watering interval, start with “7” and adjust: if the soil is still damp on day 7, cross out the 7 and write 9 or 10. The card disappears from your head once it lives on the page.
